The **Journal** is where you write down who you are becoming. It is not a blank notebook: each
entry has a *kind* taken from an idea in *Atomic Habits*, one to five short prompts, and usually one
concrete follow-up (a plan for your habit, a task, a line on your Scorecard).

It sits next to your **day notes** (the short "how did it go?" note you can add to a habit on a
given day). Day notes stay where they are and stay editable; the Journal shows them in the same
timeline, so you have one place to reread everything.

## Where to find it

- **Journal** in the sidebar (web and desktop), or **You → Journal** on phones.
- **Create → Journal entry** (the ⊕ button), or the `J` shortcut on the web; `G` then `J` opens the
  Journal.
- From **Today**: the never-miss-twice banner ("Plan the comeback"), the fresh-start banner ("Plan a
  fresh start"), the perfect-day card ("Write down the proof") and the journal nudge card.
- On a habit's page: the **Journal & notes** section, with a **Write** menu prelinked to that habit.
- On **Identity**: "+ Evidence" on each identity card, your values from the latest Integrity
  Report, and the most recent entries.

## The nine kinds

| Kind | Book idea | What you write | What it can do for you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Evidence** | Every action is a vote for the person you wish to become (ch. 2) | Proof of an identity: something you did today | Counts as evidence on the identity card (evidence is shown separately; it never adds habit votes) |
| **Notice a habit** | The Habits Scorecard, pointing-and-calling (ch. 4) | A behavior, whether it helps or hurts, what you'd say out loud | "Add to my Habits Scorecard"; for a negative one, "Break this habit" opens the editor |
| **Comeback plan** | Never miss twice (ch. 16); implementation intentions and habit stacking (ch. 5) | What got in the way, then when, where and after what you'll do it next, and the two-minute version | "Make this the habit's plan" updates the habit's cue and stack, with Undo |
| **Comeback plan** (break habit, after a slip) | Make it invisible and difficult (ch. 7 and 14) | What triggered it, which of the five cues, how to hide the cue, what friction to add | Updates the habit's environment and friction fields |
| **Goldilocks check-in** | The Goldilocks Rule, boredom, the two-minute rule (ch. 13 and 19) | Too easy, too hard or just right? | Raise or lower the daily goal (using the level-up value when one is offered), or fall back to the two-minute version |
| **Habit contract** | The habit contract and an accountability partner (ch. 17) | Your commitment, the consequence, a partner and an end date | Sign it (it locks), share it as text, and record whether you kept it |
| **Rewrite a belief** | Identity-based habits: decide who you want to be, prove it with small wins (ch. 2) | The old story, the new one, a small proof | "Make it a task" for the proof |
| **Review** (week, month or year) | James Clear's Annual Review (ch. 20) | What went well, what didn't, what you learned, and the next step | A live snapshot of the period (votes, consistency, perfect days, habits you missed twice, slips, evidence); "Make it a task" for the next step |
| **Integrity Report** | James Clear's Integrity Report (ch. 20) | Your core values, how you're living them, a higher standard | Your values appear on the Identity screen |
| **Free note** | — | Anything about who you're becoming | — |

An entry written by a newer version of Atomic, of a kind this version doesn't know, is shown as a
note and kept read-only.

## Writing an entry

- **It saves itself.** Opening the editor writes nothing; your first change creates the entry, and
  later changes are saved as you type ("Saving…" / "Saved"). Closing the editor keeps everything;
  an entry you left completely empty is discarded.
- **Required prompts never block saving.** An entry that still misses something shows an
  **Unfinished** tag on its card, and the editor marks what is left. Only a contract has to be
  complete before you can sign it.
- **The date is the day the entry is about**: the day you missed, the slip day, the day of the
  win. It defaults to today and can be any past day. A review also covers a period, which you can
  move week by week, month by month or year by year.
- **Links.** An entry can belong to an identity, to a habit, or both. Comeback plans, check-ins and
  contracts are tied to their habit for good. Deleting a habit or an identity never rewrites your
  entries; they show the old name, marked "(deleted)".
- **Pin** entries you want to keep at the top of the Journal. **Delete** is in the entry's menu,
  with Undo.

## When Atomic asks you to write

Today shows **at most one** journal nudge at a time, always with **Not now**:

| Nudge | When |
| --- | --- |
| **What triggered it?** | A break habit slipped today or yesterday and there's no comeback plan for that day yet |
| **Contract ended** | A signed contract ended in the last 30 days and you haven't said whether you kept it ("I kept it" / "I broke it") |
| **Annual Review** | December 15 – January 15, if you haven't reviewed the year |
| **Integrity Report** | June 15 – July 15 |
| **Weekly / monthly review** | Around the end of the week or month, following your **Reflection prompts** setting |
| **Collect your evidence** | An identity has been getting votes but you haven't written anything this week |

Comeback plans also show up inside the never-miss-twice banner ("Your plan: I will meditate at
07:00 on the living room couch"), so the plan is in front of you on the day it matters.

**Reflection prompts** (Settings → Preferences) chooses **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Off** for the
review nudges. On Android, the same setting schedules a local reminder at 19:00 on the last day of
the week or month (it follows the reminders switch; nothing is pushed from a server).

## The Goldilocks check-in and leveling up

The book's Goldilocks Rule says motivation peaks at the edge of your ability. Atomic uses it in two
places that agree with each other:

- After a strong month, the habit's page (and Today, in the first week of the month) suggests
  raising the goal, citing the evidence. "Not sure? Do a Goldilocks check-in" opens a check-in.
- In a check-in, "too easy" offers the same new goal when a level-up is available, and +10%
  otherwise. "Too hard" halves the goal, or points you to the two-minute version.

The habit's page asks "Still the right size?" when a habit has been strong for about a month, or
"Is it too hard right now?" when it's struggling, at most once a month.

## Search and filters

The Journal page has a search box (it ignores accents, so "licao" finds "lição"), filters by
identity, habit and kind (including **Day notes**), and groups the timeline by month.

## Privacy

- The journal lives on your device and is included in your backups (**Export backup**) and in the
  account download.
- When you're signed in, it syncs to your account like everything else. **On the server it is not
  end-to-end encrypted.** To keep it on one device, turn off **Sync journal** in the Account
  section: entries written on that device then stay there, while entries from your other devices
  still arrive. The switch is per device.
- It is **never** sent to AI, never translated, and never used for typing suggestions.
