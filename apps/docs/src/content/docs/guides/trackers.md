Some habits are done right inside the app: logging an expense, timing a meditation, writing 300
words, noting a slip. A habit with a **tracker** doesn't just get checked off: you log what you did,
and Atomic turns those entries into the day's count, so streaks, the monthly goal, identity votes
and Insights all keep working.

Every identity template comes with at least one tracker habit, marked **Logged in the app** in the
editor and in onboarding.

## How tracker habits work

- **Tap the ring to log.** On a tracker habit, the ring opens the tracker instead of toggling:
  the entry form, the timer, the writing pad, or a one-tap "I'm up" / "Going to bed" with Undo.
- **Strict.** A tracker day is done only through entries, so the "+1" / "−1" and "Reset day" actions
  are hidden on those days. Days before the tracker started keep their old check-offs.
- **Nothing to log.** The expense log has **No spending today**, which counts as done: noticing is
  the habit. It is different from **Skip**, a rest day that neither extends nor breaks the streak.
- **Votes.** Trackers that add up an amount (pages, minutes, words) give one identity vote per day,
  not one per page. The others give one vote per entry, up to the daily goal.
- **Past days.** You can log any day from the tracker's start up to today, from the calendar on the
  habit's page. There are no future entries.
- **Summaries.** The habit's page shows a tracker panel with stats, a chart and lists for the week,
  month or year, and the latest entries ("See all" expands them, month by month).

### Adding, switching or removing a tracker

- In the habit editor, the *Make it easy* step asks **How will you log it?**: a **Simple check-off**
  or one of the trackers that fit the habit (slip journal only for break habits).
- A plain habit whose name looks like a tracker shows a **Make it interactive** card on its page.
- **Tracker settings** (habit menu) changes the options; **Switch to simple check-off** turns it
  off. Your history and entries are kept, and you can turn the tracker back on any time.
- Turning a tracker on for an existing habit starts **today**: "Your history so far stays as it is."
- Trackers measured in days or by a deadline (expense, workout, wake-up, bedtime…) have a daily
  goal of 1, so the monthly goal can't be made up with extra entries; trackers that add up amounts
  or count entries can, up to twice the daily goal.

## The twelve trackers

| Tracker | Records | The day counts | Templates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expense log** | Amount, currency, category, note | Done when you log an expense or "No spending today" | Log expenses (I am good with money) |
| **Reading log** | Pages (or minutes), book, bookmark page, a quote | Pages (or minutes) read | Read 10 pages (I am a reader) |
| **Workout log** | Activity, minutes, distance, effort, note | Done when you log a workout | Work out; Take a walk (I am an active person) |
| **Timer** | A timed session: duration, topic, note | Minutes (or sessions) | Meditate; Practice a language; Practice an instrument; Tidy up for 5 minutes |
| **Writing pad** | Text written in the app (or words written elsewhere), title, project | Words (or entries) | Write 300 words (I am a writer); Journal (I am calm and present) |
| **Wake-up check-in** | The time you got up | Done when you're up by the target time plus grace | Wake up at 6:30 (I am an early riser) |
| **Bedtime log** | The time you went to bed, when you woke, how you slept | Done when you're in bed by the target time | In bed by 11pm (I take care of my body) |
| **Plan tomorrow** | 1–5 priorities for the next day, a reflection | Done when you plan; each line becomes a task | Plan tomorrow (I am organized) |
| **Connection log** | Who, how (call, video, visit, message, letter), minutes, what you talked about | Done when you reach out | Call someone I love (I am present with the people I love) |
| **Slip journal** | For break habits: what set it off (one of the five cues), a note, what it cost | Each slip counts as a slip | Impulse buying (cost in money); Doomscrolling (cost in time) |
| **Photo journal** | 1–4 photos and a caption, or a caption alone when photos are optional | Done when you add one | Progress photo (I am an active person); Eat vegetables as a meal log (I take care of my body) |
| **Number log** | A value with your unit (push-ups, kg, glasses) and a note | The sum of the day, or the last value | Any build habit |

### Expense log

Amounts are stored exactly (in cents), in one of 16 currencies (Real, US dollar, euro and others),
defaulting to your region's. Categories come built in (food, groceries, transport, home, bills,
health, fun, shopping, education, gifts, subscriptions, pets, other); you can hide them or add your
own. Set an optional **monthly budget** and the habit's headline reads "R$ 320 left this month" (or
"over budget"), with your pace for the month. The summary shows totals, the daily average, a
comparison with the previous period, no-spend days, spending by category and your largest
expenses. Atomic never converts currencies: entries in another currency are listed apart.

### Reading log and shelf

Say "I'm on page 134" and Atomic works out how many pages you read since last time, or use
+5 / +10 / +20. Your **shelf** keeps the book you're reading (with a progress bar and "At this pace
you'll finish on…"), the next ones, and the books finished this year with your rating. Reaching the
last page asks whether you finished the book. Quotes you save show up as "Your highlights".

### Workout log

Pick an activity (run, walk, strength, cycling, swim, yoga, HIIT, sport, dance, climb, or your own),
then optionally minutes, distance (km or mi) and effort from Easy to Max. The summary counts
workouts, total time and distance, and keeps your personal records.

### Timer

A countdown (the template's two-minute meditation, 15 minutes of a language…) or an open stopwatch.
It keeps time even if you leave the screen; at zero a bell rings (on Android, a notification if the
app is in the background), and you can **Keep going**. After a session it can ask what you learned
or how you felt. "Log time manually" records a session done elsewhere.

### Writing pad

A distraction-free page with a live word count and progress ring ("214 / 300 words"). Drafts are
kept on the device every second and saved to the entry when you're done, leave, or every minute.
A page holds up to 16,000 characters; then it continues on a new page. "On this day" shows what you
wrote a week, a month and years ago.

### Wake-up check-in and bedtime log

One tap records the time: **I'm up** (before 15:00) or **Going to bed** (18:00 to 04:59; after
midnight it counts for the night before). The target time plus a grace period is saved with each
entry, so changing your target later doesn't rewrite past days. The bedtime log asks the next
morning when you woke up and how you slept, and once it has enough nights it tells you how much
more you sleep when you're in bed on time.

### Plan tomorrow

Write one to five priorities for tomorrow (on a Friday you can also pick the next weekday). Each line becomes a task for
that day; editing the plan updates the open tasks, and deleting it removes them. The summary shows
how much of what you planned you actually finished.

### Connection log

Who you reached out to, how and about what. Keep a **circle** of people with how often you want to
be in touch, and the summary shows who's overdue ("Grandma: 23 days since you talked").

### Slip journal

For break habits. **Just log it** records a slip in one tap; add, if you want, which of the book's
five cues set it off (a time of day, a place, something you'd just done, a feeling, other people),
a note, and what it cost in money or time. The summary shows clean days, what slips cost you this
month, the most common cue and the time of day slips happen. After a slip, Atomic offers to
**Reflect** in a journal comeback plan, prefilled with what you logged.

### Photo journal

Take or pick up to four photos. With **progress mode** (the Progress photo template) the summary
puts your first photo next to the latest. With photos **optional** (the meal log), a caption alone
counts, and the action reads "Add photo or note".

### Number log

Any number with your own unit, decimals, and whether up or down is good. The day counts the sum of
the values, or the last one (for a weight, say). The summary shows the latest value, the change
over the period, the lowest, highest and average.

## Photos

- Photos are resized and **re-encoded on your device**, which strips their location and other
  hidden metadata. Entries only hold references to them, so syncing stays light.
- They live on the device that took them. Signed in, with **Back up photos** on (the default) and a
  server that offers photo backup, they are uploaded to your account after their entry is synced,
  and your other devices download them. A photo that isn't on a device yet shows "Photo not on this
  device yet".
- Photo storage depends on your plan (100 MB on Free, 5 GB on Pro). When it's full, new photos stay
  on the device.
- **Export backup** (JSON) does not include photos; **Download account data** does.
- On the web, photos need a browser with IndexedDB and a secure connection (HTTPS); the claude.ai
  artifact version has no photos.

## Older versions of Atomic

A version of Atomic from before trackers still shows tracker habits as done or not done, because
each entry also writes the day's count. If it checks a tracker habit off by hand, a newer device
corrects the count from the entries. A tracker added by a newer version than yours is shown
read-only, with "Update the app to log it".
